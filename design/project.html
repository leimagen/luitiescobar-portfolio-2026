Motion Design/2.5D

Paper Cut — a 2.5D story about slow mornings

A 45-second animated short built from layered paper textures, parallax depth and hand-timed motion.

Client
Nébula Coffee
Year
2026
Role
Art direction, Animation
Tools
After Effects, Cinema 4D
128

The brief

Nébula wanted a campaign piece that felt handmade and warm, far from the glossy 3D look of their competitors. The film had to work as a 45s hero and as 6s loops for social.

  • One hero film (16:9) + six vertical cut-downs
  • Palette restricted to the brand's four paper tones
  • Delivery in three weeks

Process

Every layer was scanned from real cut paper, then rebuilt in 3D space so the camera could move between planes. Timing was keyed by hand to keep a slightly imperfect, stop-motion rhythm.

“Motion should feel like it was touched, not computed.”

The final grade added subtle grain and light leaks to sell the analog feel.
